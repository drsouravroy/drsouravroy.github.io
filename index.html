Research At A Glance

Connecting protein structure and dynamics to disease mechanism and therapeutic design

Dr. Roy’s research brings together computational biophysics, structural biology, and infectious disease biology to study how proteins adopt structure, transition between functional states, and interact in complex biological environments.

The broader aim is to connect protein dynamics, pathogen biology, complement evasion, and structure-guided therapeutic discovery with emerging AI-assisted workflows for peptide and molecule design.

Mechanistic protein science can provide a direct path from molecular understanding to therapeutic hypothesis.

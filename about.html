Academic Biography

A quantitative path into molecular mechanism and disease biology

Sourav Roy, Ph.D. is a Research Assistant Professor in the Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biophysics at Kansas State University. His academic background began in physics and evolved toward life science through a sustained interest in how molecular systems encode biological function.

His research focuses on computational biophysics, protein structure and dynamics, and the mechanistic interpretation of biological systems relevant to disease. This includes work on protein folding, conformational change, molecular interactions, and the structural basis of host-pathogen behavior.

A major scientific theme in his work is the study of Lyme disease-related protein systems, especially Borrelia burgdorferi proteins associated with virulence and complement evasion. These questions connect directly to broader interests in infectious disease mechanisms, structure-guided discovery, and AI-enabled peptide and small-molecule discovery strategies.

Research Programs & Scientific Directions

Scientific themes organized around mechanism, design, and biological relevance

Research directions rather than claimed funded projects

These scientific directions define the larger research program across host-pathogen biology, complement systems, protein dynamics, and structure-guided therapeutic discovery.

Structural Mechanisms of Host-Pathogen Interaction

  • Research QuestionHow do pathogen proteins interact with host systems to support survival and virulence?
  • Scientific ApproachCombine structural interpretation, interaction analysis, and computational modeling to define molecular mechanism.
  • Potential ImpactImproved mechanistic understanding of disease-associated protein systems and new therapeutic entry points.
  • Related MethodsStructural modeling, interface analysis, molecular dynamics.

Complement Evasion and Therapeutic Targeting

  • Research QuestionHow do microbial proteins manipulate complement pathways, and how can those interactions be therapeutically addressed?
  • Scientific ApproachStudy complement-associated proteins, protease interactions, and inhibitor concepts with structural and biophysical reasoning.
  • Potential ImpactSharper therapeutic hypotheses in complement biology and infectious disease.
  • Related MethodsBinding-site analysis, inhibitor interpretation, complement-focused structural studies.

Protein Dynamics in Folding and Disease Mechanisms

  • Research QuestionHow do folding pathways, intermediate states, and conformational change contribute to biological outcome?
  • Scientific ApproachAnalyze energy landscapes, unfolding behavior, and dynamic transitions relevant to protein function.
  • Potential ImpactDeeper understanding of molecular behavior that supports mechanism-driven biology.
  • Related MethodsMolecular dynamics, protein stability analysis, network-based structural interpretation.

AI-Assisted Peptide Discovery

  • Research QuestionHow can AI-guided workflows help identify peptide candidates for defined targets?
  • Scientific ApproachUse computational prioritization and mechanistic filtering to support peptide hypothesis generation.
  • Potential ImpactFaster and more targeted identification of peptide discovery opportunities.
  • Related MethodsAI-assisted ranking, structure-aware screening, target-focused analysis.

Target-Specific Small Molecule Design

  • Research QuestionHow can computational design strategies be tailored to specific protein targets and interfaces?
  • Scientific ApproachCombine structural context, binding-site information, and automated AI models for candidate design.
  • Potential ImpactStronger target-specific design logic for early-stage discovery.
  • Related MethodsDocking, pocket analysis, AI-assisted molecule generation.

Protein-Protein Interaction Mapping in Bacterial Systems

  • Research QuestionWhich bacterial protein interactions are most informative for mechanism and functional adaptation?
  • Scientific ApproachMap interactions and infer functional logic in non-cholera Vibrio and related systems.
  • Potential ImpactNew insight into bacterial biology and network-level functional organization.
  • Related MethodsProtein-protein interaction analysis, sequence-function interpretation, structural context mapping.
